## ○‎‎ ‎ ‎‎ How to play ?

> I haven't created the rules. Maybe, through the natural process of informations being conveyed on the internet, i may have gotten inspired by a variant of the original game, or maybe something completely different that simply took the same name.

You are given a big board of Tic-Tac-Toe, except, each square contains a Tic-Tac-Toe board itself called `sub-games`.
	Each time a player plays in a `sub-game`, the next player forced to play in its corresponding `sub-game.`.
 
If a `sub-game` is won, the entire square is owned by the player who won in it.
	If the next player HAS to play in a `sub-game` that has already been claimed, they get to chose where to play next.
 
The first one to place 3 `sub-games` in a row (according to Tic-Tac-Toe rules) wins the entire game.
